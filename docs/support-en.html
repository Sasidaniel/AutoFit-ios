How can we help?

For support, bug reports and privacy questions, email us below. Include your app version, iOS version and reproduction steps. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or medical information; hide personal information in screenshots.

Getting started and permissions

An AutoFit account is not required. Review a proposed plan before activating it. The interface supports Hebrew and English; iPhone voice commands support Hebrew and require microphone and speech permissions. Manual set entry remains available. Health, music and biometric lock are separately authorized features.

Your records

Use “My data” in the menu to export JSON or erase local records. Deletion does not automatically erase Health records or previously shared files. This is a general fitness tool, not an emergency service, diagnosis or medical advice.
