AutoFit support

Your personal workout partner.

How can we help?

For support, bug reports and privacy questions: autofitrainer@gmail.com

For a bug report, include the app version, iOS version and the steps leading to the issue. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment details or medical information. Hide personal information in screenshots.

This is not an emergency service, diagnosis or medical advice.

Getting started

An AutoFit account is not required. Choose an existing plan or build a proposal around your goal, time, training environment and equipment. Review and confirm before activating a plan; proposals do not automatically replace your existing selection.

Voice control and permissions

The interface supports Hebrew and English; voice commands currently support Hebrew in the iOS app. They require microphone and speech-recognition permissions. If listening stops, check the displayed status and recovery action. Manual entry remains available. Change permissions in iOS Settings.

Your records

Workout records are stored locally. Use “My data” in the menu to export a JSON file or erase AutoFit records. Exports may contain measurements and photos: store them in a destination you trust. Deletion does not erase previously shared files or workouts already saved to Apple Health.

New sensitive-data iCloud writes are disabled. Older copies are not automatically deleted; legacy export or recovery requires a separate explicit action. Saving on your device is not confirmation of an external backup.

Apple Health and Apple Music

Saving workouts to Health requires separate permission. Later AutoFit edits do not automatically update workouts already sent to Health. For music controls, first choose a song or playlist in your player and grant access when requested; music and subscriptions are not included.
